# Employee Attrition Analysis with Python

This project analyzes employee attrition using Python, pandas, and matplotlib.

The goal is to identify key factors associated with employee turnover and provide practical recommendations for HR decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Understand the overall employee attrition rate
- Compare attrition across departments and job roles
- Analyze the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Compare income, age, and tenure between employees who stayed and left
- Explore job satisfaction, work-life balance, and business travel
- Provide business insights and HR recommendations

## Tools Used

- Python
- pandas
- matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Key Findings

- The overall employee attrition rate is approximately 16.1%.
- Employees who work overtime have a much higher attrition rate than employees who do not.
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate among job roles.
- Employees who leave have lower average monthly income than employees who stay.
- Younger employees and employees with shorter tenure tend to show higher attrition.
- Business travel, work-life balance, and job satisfaction are associated with differences in attrition rates.

## Business Recommendations

- Review overtime policies and workload distribution.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-risk job roles.
- Review compensation levels for higher-risk employee groups.
- Strengthen onboarding and career development support for newer employees.
- Regularly monitor employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
